#  QuickBite Crisis Recovery Analysis  
**End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python | SQL | Power BI**

---

##  Project Overview
QuickBite Express is a food delivery startup that experienced a **major operational crisis in June 2025**, leading to a sharp decline in orders, revenue, customer satisfaction, and delivery performance.

This project analyzes the **root causes of the crisis** and provides **data-driven recovery recommendations** using Python, SQL, and Power BI.  
The focus is on **business problem solving and decision support**, not just visualization.

---

##  Business Objective
- Measure the impact of the crisis on orders, revenue, customers, and delivery performance  
- Identify **where and why** the decline occurred  
- Compare **pre-crisis vs crisis** performance  
- Provide actionable recovery insights for leadership  

---

##  Analytics Approach
- **Python:** Data cleaning, EDA, trend analysis, pre vs crisis comparison  
- **SQL:** Business queries, performance metrics, root cause analysis  
- **Power BI:** Executive dashboard with KPI-driven storytelling  

Each tool has a clearly defined, non-overlapping role.

---

##  Key Insights
- Significant drop in orders and revenue during the crisis phase  
- Certain cities experienced a **30–40% decline** in order volume  
- Delivery delays increased sharply  
- On-time delivery percentage dropped  
- High-volume restaurants were most affected  
- Customer ratings declined during the crisis  

**Conclusion:**  
The crisis was primarily driven by **internal operational failures**, not external market conditions.

---

##  Business Recommendations
- Optimize delivery routing and fleet availability  
- Improve ETA prediction accuracy  
- Launch customer win-back campaigns  
- Temporarily reduce restaurant commission pressure  
- Prioritize fast-preparation restaurants  
- Strengthen peak-hour delivery coverage
